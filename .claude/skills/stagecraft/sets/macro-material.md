# Set: macro-material

**Point of view.** The material is the story. The camera lives millimetres from the surface and the page changes scale as it scrolls.

**Core axes.** Extreme close-up, a texture that is real (scanned or photographed, not noise), scale as a verb.
**Free axes.** Material, lens, colour, whether the camera ever pulls back to the whole.

| Rule     | Realisation                                                                              |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light    | Raking light that reveals relief; direction is a script variable                         |
| Ground   | The material itself                                                                      |
| Lens     | Macro, very shallow depth; focus pulls are the cuts                                      |
| Mass     | Fine: small settles, dust, the give of a surface                                         |

**Failure mode.** A procedural noise texture pretending to be material, and a camera that never leaves macro so the product is never seen.

**Exemplars.** None captured yet.
