# Set: daylight-location

**Point of view.** The actor in the world it is used in, lit by the sun at an hour you can name. The page's clock is the sun.

**Core axes.** A real-feeling place (not a backdrop), a sun direction that changes with the script, shadows cast by the set onto the actor and back.
**Free axes.** Place, season, lens, time model, how much of the place is shown.

| Rule     | Realisation                                                                              |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light    | The sun as a directional light whose angle is a script variable, plus sky fill           |
| Ground   | Pavement, grass, a table, a floor: from the product's real use                           |
| Lens     | A natural field of view; depth of field from distance, not a slider                      |
| Mass     | Full, with environmental contact: dust, grass bend, reflection                            |

**Failure mode.** A stock photo as a backdrop with a cut-out pasted over it: the actor shares no light with the place.

**Exemplars.** None captured yet.
