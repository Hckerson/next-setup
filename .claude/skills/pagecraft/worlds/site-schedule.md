# Site schedule

Building sites, collection rounds and trades in people's homes. The world's rule is the sequence: the job is a programme of tasks and crews, and everything on site is marked so the next trade can read it.

## Artefacts

| Artefact | Can supply |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Programme charts (bars per trade, the critical path, milestones as diamonds) | A timeline device, a milestone mark |
| Punch and snag lists with photos and pins on a plan | Row anatomy (location, item, trade, state), a pin on a plan |
| Site marking (spray paint and flagging tape in utility colours, survey stakes) | An accent system where each colour has a utility's job |
| Hi-vis, hard hats by role, site hoarding | A high-chroma accent confined to warning, role colours |
| Delivery and weighbridge tickets, skip and bin tags | A ticket shape, gross, tare and net as a numeric style |
| Job sheets and quotes from the van (trades) | A short form, a signature on completion, line items |
| Site boards (contractor, permit, emergency contacts) | A plate layout, a signage face |

## Standards

- **APWA uniform colour code** for marking buried utilities (red power, yellow gas, orange telecoms, blue water, green sewer, white proposed).
- **EN ISO 20471** high-visibility colours; **ISO 7010** site safety signs.
- Scheduling conventions (critical path, float, percent complete).
- Waste transfer notes and weighbridge records where the jurisdiction uses them.

## Archetype pressure

Console (programme and map together), rows (tasks, defects, collections), marks (open, in progress, blocked, signed off), forms (a completion signed on a phone with gloves on: large targets).

## The category template

A stock photo of a smiling worker in a hard hat in front of a crane, orange and black or yellow and black stripes, a blueprint background, a van wrap with a phone number, "quality you can trust".
