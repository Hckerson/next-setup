# Survey map

Fields, forests, fisheries, freight routes and climate. The world's rule is that place is measured against a datum, and every mark on the map has a legend.

## Artefacts

| Artefact | Can supply |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Topographic and ordnance maps (contours, hatching, a legend, grid references) | A line language, the legend as a status vocabulary, a grid as the ground |
| Nautical and aeronautical charts (soundings, compass rose, magenta overprint) | A one-hue overprint accent with a job, numeric annotations in the field |
| Cadastral and parcel plans | Shapes (parcels as cells), identifiers first in a row, boundary rules |
| Field notebooks and survey books (waterproof paper, pencil, ruled columns) | A paper ground, a handwritten or pencil-weight annotation voice, rows |
| Soil colour charts and crop calendars | A neutral system taken from earth, a season band as a timeline |
| Shipping documents (waybills, bills of lading, container codes, pallet labels) | Row anatomy, check digits, a label system with barcodes |
| Weather station logs and buoy readings | Small multiples, observation time first |

## Standards

- **USGS and national mapping symbol sets**, **IHO S-4** chart specifications, OS grid references or what3words-style addressing as numeric styles.
- **Munsell soil colour notation** as a source for neutrals.
- **ISO 6346** container codes and **GS1** logistics labels for row identifiers.
- **GHG Protocol** scopes for carbon figures.

## Archetype pressure

Patterns (a map with its legend is a pattern, not a picture), rows (parcels, loads, catches), console (map plus list, kept in step), marks (route and field states).

## The category template

A drone photo of green fields or a wheat close-up at golden hour, a leaf icon, green-to-teal gradients, a generic slippy map with blue pins, a truck on a highway at dusk, a globe with arcs.

## Stage notes
