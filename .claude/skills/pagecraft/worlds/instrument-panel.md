# Instrument panel

Plants, grids, wells, networks and control rooms. Operators watch for hours and act in seconds, so the world's own rule is that normal is quiet and abnormal is loud.

## Artefacts

| Artefact | Can supply |
| -------- | ---------- |
| High-performance HMI screens (grey process graphics, analogue bars with normal bands) | Neutral (a working grey, not a mood), density, the rule that colour means abnormal |
| Annunciator panels (backlit alarm windows in a grid, first-out flashing) | Status vocabulary, a signature grid device, the alarm motion model |
| Engraved laminate labels and equipment tags (white core on a coloured face) | Type voice (engraved sans, often condensed), shape (small hard plates), a label system |
| Strip-chart and circular chart recorders | Trend rendering, gridded paper as a ground, a numeric style |
| Electrical cabinets and enclosures (painted steel, DIN rail, wiring ducts) | Neutral (cabinet grey), elevation by panel and inset, chrome as a rail |
| Lockout and tagout tags, permit-to-work forms | Status words, a hard-edged warning tone confined to danger |
| Shift logbooks and handover sheets | Row anatomy (time first), the record of who acted |

## Standards

- **ISA-101** (HMI design): grey or muted backgrounds, colour reserved for abnormal conditions, levels of display from overview to detail.
- **ISA-18.2 / IEC 62682** (alarm management): priorities, states (unacknowledged, acknowledged, shelved, suppressed, returned to normal), and why each needs a distinct form, not only a colour.
- **IEC 60073**: meanings of indicator colours (red emergency, yellow abnormal, green normal, blue mandatory action).
- **NAMUR NE 107**: four device diagnostics (failure, function check, out of specification, maintenance required), each with its own symbol.
- **ISO 7010 and ANSI Z535**: safety signs and safety colours.

## Archetype pressure

Marks (alarm and device states, with shape and word), rows (event and alarm lists, time first), console (overview to detail), motion (an alarm's flash is meaning, not decoration; obey the three-flashes rule).

## The category template

A dark navy dashboard of neon cyan gauges and donut charts, a glowing "Industry 4.0" hero with a robot arm or a turbine at sunset, hexagon patterns, a smart-factory isometric illustration. Every value lit, so nothing is abnormal.
