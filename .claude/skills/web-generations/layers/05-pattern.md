# Layer 5: Pattern

`components/patterns/`. Domain-free compositions of primitives: stat cell, stat strip, data table, page intro, empty state, key-value list, filter bar, skeletons.

**Upstream:** Token, Scale class, Primitive. A recurring tell here is usually inherited from these layers: trace it there and fix it once, not once per file here.

## Tells

A tell fires on a composition the template made, decided by nobody. The same composition chosen on purpose and recorded in the brief (a centred intro, a filled container) is a register, not a tell.

## Floor versus baseline

Fourth-generation patterns are clean and reusable, and each is the library's: the same card, the same intro cluster, the same table. They lack state coverage (an empty state per situation, errors isolated to their region, loading that matches the final geometry), tables that align and mark their figures, and a container chosen per pattern. The baseline asks for those; how dense, centred or contained the patterns are is the register's.

## Baseline

Every register meets these. Counts are of the 18 exemplars (`references/classification-ledger.md`, section 05).

- **PAT-B1. Tables align and mark figures.** Numeric columns are right-aligned with their unit, unknown values are marked rather than left blank, sort headers are buttons, and the table is a real `<table>` with a caption. `(S 4/18, thin)` Vercel, Stripe, Mercury, Apple Store iOS. Vercel's em dash for unknown and its seven-day relative time are its instance.
- **PAT-B2. An empty state per situation.** Each situation (blank, no results, permission, error) has its own empty state, which says what the entity is for and offers at most one primary next step. Casing follows the brief. `(S 3/18)` Vercel, Linear, Wise ("Nothing to show here yet / Your transactions will show here.").
- **PAT-B3. Errors isolated, with retry.** An error is isolated to its region and offers a retry; the rest of the page keeps working. `(S 1/18, thin)` Vercel. Its copyable request id is a developer-tool instance.
- **PAT-B4. Skeletons match the final geometry.** A loading placeholder has the final layout's shapes and count, not generic bars. `(S 2/18, thin)` Aesop's product silhouettes in the image slots; Apple's product-viewer loader tokens.
- **PAT-B5. App: fixed title positions.** App titles and their actions sit in the same positions across pages. Which header shape (a title with tabs and actions, or a breadcrumb with icon actions) is the register's. `(S 2/18, thin)` Linear's refresh; Duolingo's core-tabs refresh puts titles in a consistent left-aligned position.
- **PAT-B6. Marketing: one primary action in the intro.** A page intro offers one primary action. Alignment is the register's: Duolingo's centred landing is a recorded composition, not a tell. `(J, thin)`
- **PAT-B7. Marketing: intro tension on two axes.** The intro headline and its support text differ on at least two axes (size, family or cut, weight, tracking, leading). A 3x size jump is the register's, and any multiplier must name its viewport. `(S)` Aesop, Things, Duolingo and TE pass under 3x. See TOK-B12.
- **PAT-B8. Marketing: claims carry evidence.** A claim carries its evidence in the same typographic system as the claim, and a footnote marker is wired to its disclosure with `aria-describedby`. `(S 2/18, thin)` Mercury's counter-generated footnotes (14 references); Wise's rate, fees and savings in its hero calculator.

## Techniques

Register-neutral methods, recommended, not required.

- **Stat cells.** Let the value dominate its label, give it its unit, and show freshness where the data ages. Seen in Stripe's stat cell with an "Updated" footer, Apple's large stat numerals and Wise's calculator figures; the full anatomy is measured in one exemplar, so it is not yet a criterion.
- **Content per entity type.** Design the content structure for each entity rather than one template for all (Stripe Press). One exemplar.
- **Announcing changes.** Announce filter and result-count changes with `aria-live`, and quote a single search term verbatim in a no-results state (Vercel).
- **Keeping skeletons honest.** A test that renders the skeleton beside the final component keeps the two geometries in step.

## Token roles this layer needs

Row height and density steps, header height, numeric alignment and tabular figures, unit, delta and freshness text roles, the recorded container set (rule, fill, border, open space), empty and error copy slots, skeleton colour and shimmer timing.
