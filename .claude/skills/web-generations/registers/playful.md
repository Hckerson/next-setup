# Register: Playful and bold

**Point of view.** The interface is a toy with rules. Saturated colour with jobs, physical feedback and characters that carry state, held together by a small strict set of shapes, weights and type roles that keeps the noise legible. Two readings meet the bar: a toy that rewards each action (Duolingo) and a toy that sets a mood (Headspace). In both, the chrome stays out of the colour's way so the colour can shout.

**Exemplars.** Duolingo (playful in the product, rewarding each tap), Headspace (playful in the product, setting a mood; 2023 Apple Design Award), Figma (bold in brand and marketing; its app is a quiet tool). Evidence: `references/registers/trust-playful.md`, `references/registers/playful-headspace.md`.

**Suits.** Learning, habits, games, wellbeing, creative tools' marketing, consumer products that must motivate. **Strains** for expert-density work. It strains less in regulated or clinical contexts than its loudest exemplar suggests: Headspace runs it into therapy and coaching by keeping the chrome quiet.

## Layer by layer

| Layer        | Realisation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Token        | Each hue has a job and a designed dark value. A (fill, lip) colour pair is Duolingo's instance; a neutral lip on one action is Headspace's                                                                                                                                                               |
| Scale class  | Variable axes as type roles (Figma), or a constant tracking across one face's roles (Headspace)                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Primitive    | Hover, press and disabled are distinct: press visibly changes depth or fill. Travel by the lip height is Duolingo's instance; removing the lip without travel is Headspace's. | Mark         | Marks with counts (streaks, gems, hearts) or with moods (faces); status by illustrated glyph plus colour plus word                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Row and list | One selection colour shared by nav items, tiles and list items; the container recipe may differ by component                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Section      | Colour pairs or full-bleed colour fields per section; maximal schemes reserved for emphasis                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Signature devices

| Property               | Exemplar instance                                                               | Derive your own from                                                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Controls feel physical | Duolingo's lip and key travel; Headspace's lip on the one primary action        | A physical object in the product's world that users press, flip or stack |
| Characters carry state | Duolingo's characters react to taps; Headspace's faces carry the content's mood | The product's own subject (a mascot only if it can carry state)          |
| Colour has a job       | Duolingo green go, blue selected; Headspace blue act, orange now, green done    | The meanings the domain already gives to colour                          |

## Failure mode

Rainbow fills, a rounded font in bold everywhere, bouncy overshoot on every element, emoji as icons, confetti, and a lip on everything including cards and inputs. It copies the costume and drops the rules: the lip without its press logic, colours without jobs, a mascot as decoration instead of a state machine. The Headspace version: orange smiley blobs on every card, peach and lavender gradients, everything pill-shaped on a beige "wellness" ground, faces that never change expression. The Figma version: colourful blobs on white with a black pill CTA and no colour-pair logic.

A register-wide device such as the lip is not a "deliberate exception" confined to one place; it is the register. The second-generation tells about raised edges re-scope accordingly in `layers/03-primitive.md` and `layers/07-row.md`.

## Staying in the register without cloning its exemplars

The core axes (hues with jobs, characters or physical feedback carrying state, expressiveness where the content lives) will match the exemplars; that is what makes a build read as this register. The six free axes are where Duolingo, Headspace and Figma are instances. On at least three of them, the brief chooses an instance that differs from every exemplar's and records the reason from the domain.

Steer away from both in-product exemplars' instances: Duolingo's rounded heavy face, 12 to 16px radii, 17 to 19px body, 50px controls and same-hue lip with travel; Headspace's mixed-case grotesque at constant negative tracking, pill controls in 16px cards, warm-grey tonal cards, horizon arc and closed-eyed face. Neither supplies a second density instance, so density needs a domain reason of its own.

Example: a chunky slab serif for display over a plain grotesque body, zero-radius plates with thick ink outlines, and a top bar carrying the session's progress, where Duolingo is rounded with a loud sidebar and Headspace is pill-shaped with a quiet rail, while the hues with jobs, the state-carrying feedback and the expressive content stay.

## Exemplar gaps (do not copy)

- Duolingo web: no visible focus on buttons (outlines removed globally), no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling in 172 CSS chunks, white on its green 2.09:1, nav labels 4.48:1.
- Headspace: orange as text and state at 2.73:1; input borders at 1.34:1 and choice tiles at 1.09:1; hover identical to press on most buttons; disabled by inconsistent opacities; token pipeline leaks (an undefined spacing token, invalid font-size values).
- Headspace shows the register can meet the accessibility bar in product: action contrast 5.31:1, a 2px focus outline at a 4px offset, reduced-motion handling. Copy that, not the gaps.

## Evidence gaps

Duolingo keyboard focus and reduced-motion behaviour in the running app, lesson feedback sheets, and the Figma editor were not captured; Duolingo renders nothing without its scripts, so offline measurement failed. Headspace's iOS tap reactions on its characters are UNVERIFIED.
