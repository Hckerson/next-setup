## Rules used

**Exemplars (18).** Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Raycast, Apple, Press (Stripe Press), Pudding, Notion, Things, TE (Teenage Engineering), Nothing, Ableton, Bloomberg, Mercury, Wise, Duolingo, Figma, Aesop.

**Register keys.** DEV developer-tool minimal; EDI editorial; WRM warm utility; TAC tactile and hardware; IND industrial and technical; TRU trust and finance; PLY playful and bold; LUX luxury and restraint.

**Counting.**

- A count names only exemplars whose property a source measured or documented. Exemplars nobody measured are left out of both "shows" and "breaks".
- Bloomberg values in TI come from a third-party reading (BB-3P) and are UNVERIFIED. They appear as "(+Bloomberg, UNVERIFIED)" and are not counted. MC has a first-party rendered capture of bloomberg.com/professional; those values are counted.
- Apple is one exemplar across FE (iPhone) and LX (Mac, Vision Pro, AirPods).
- MC caveat: computed `transition-timing-function` is `ease` on every element by default, so MC's `ease` counts measure element count, not a choice. Press and Duolingo render partially in MC.
- Contrast ratios are the sources' own, except two marked "computed for this ledger" (WCAG relative luminance, alpha flattened on white).

**Classes.**

- QUALITY: register-independent. Shown across registers; no premium exemplar breaks it on purpose. A break that is a defect is listed but does not make the property a register.
- REGISTER: premium exemplars split along register lines and the break is a deliberate, consistent style choice. Where a register-independent core survives, the last column gives it as "Baseline residue".
- CONFLICT: evidence splits inside a register, or the break could be a defect. Resolved in the CONFLICTS section.
- UNKNOWN: a stylistic property documented by fewer than two exemplars, or a method that cannot be observed from shipped output.
- Functional rules (state coverage, keyboard, error isolation, metadata) with thin evidence and no break are classed QUALITY and marked "thin".
- Project rules (single source of truth, `lib/data`) are architecture, not style. They are noted under the table, not classed.

**Tell verdicts.**

- Keep: no premium exemplar shows the symptom deliberately.
- Re-scope: the tell is valid, but its symptom or detector also catches a deliberate register choice. The new wording makes it fire on an undecided default.
- Fix: the tell contradicts the skill's own Target text, or its detector fires on most exemplars.

## CONFLICTS

### C1. Overlay enter and exit, and "exit faster than enter"

**Rows.** 03.8b, 08.8, 08.9 (scrim fade); tells PRI-G4-5 and LST-G4-4; 03 Check 3 ("Equal durations are a gap"); GEN calibration tell 4.

**Side A: designed enter and exit, exit faster.**

- Mercury app: appear .2s on `cubic-bezier(0,0,.2,1)`; disappear .15s on `cubic-bezier(.35,0,1,1)` [TP].
- Vercel: overlay enter .3s, scale .96, overshoot; exit not documented [FE].
- Apple: flyout curtain .32s after an 80ms delay; flyout close delay .12s [LX, FE].

**Side B: premium, but exit not faster, or no measured motion.**

- Notion: fade-out .2s, slower than fade-in .15s [EW].
- Mercury marketing: scaleIn .25s equals scaleOut .25s [TP].
- Linear: highlight fade-in 0s, fade-out .15s, so the enter is instant and the exit eases [FE].
- TE: toast enter and leave both .2s; modal has no transition found [TI].
- Aesop: nav flyout toggles `display: none` with no transition rule; live motion UNVERIFIED [LX].

**Working proposal:** the baseline needs a designed enter and exit; "exit faster than enter" is a register choice.

**Verdict: agree, with one amendment.**

- "Exit faster" is held by only one measured exemplar (Mercury app). Notion and Linear run it the other way on purpose, and Mercury's own marketing runs it equal.
- TE's modal and Aesop's flyout show premium overlays with no measured transition. If captures confirm them (TI capture 3, LX "Aesop desktop nav flyout"), an instant cut has to be allowed as a recorded decision; a hardware register could plausibly want a switch-like cut.

**Proposed baseline:** "Every overlay's enter and exit are designed and recorded in the brief. Nothing pops by omission. An instant cut is allowed only as a recorded decision. The exit never delays the user's next action, scrims fade with their layer, and under reduced motion the duration stays above zero where JS waits on the transition (03.9)."

**Moves to register:** "exit faster than enter" (DEV and TRU app tempo), the .3s and .96 values (DEV), and enter-from-origin for anchored popovers (DEV).

**Edits this needs:** fix PRI-G4-5 and LST-G4-4 as in their rows, and delete "Equal durations are a gap" from 03 Check 3 and 08.8.

### C2. Focus: two-layer ring versus any visible designed focus

**Rows.** 03.2; tell PRI-G4-2; XC accessibility baseline.

**Side A: two-layer ring (a gap, then a ring).** Vercel, Apple, Aesop, Linear (offset as the gap) [FE, LX]; Notion on image-like controls [EW]; Mercury (offset 4) and Figma (dashed, offset 4) [TP]. LX scores it 4 of 4 across Aesop, Apple, Linear and Vercel.

**Side B: single layer, premium.**

- Wise: 2px ink outline at offset 0, 19.23:1; a 3px inset ring on inputs [TP].
- Nothing: border swaps to yellow, plus a transparent outline for forced colours [TI].
- Ableton: border turns #000; links get a blue border plus a 3px glow at 50% [TI].
- Things: 2px ring at 50 to 75% alpha, no gap [EW].
- TI found "never a two-layer ring in the evidence" for its four sites.

**Defects, not register.**

- Duolingo: global `outline: none`; one rule in 172 chunks [TP].
- TE: no `:focus-visible` rule found (UNVERIFIED) [TI].
- Aesop: no focus without JS (inferred) [LX].
- Pudding: focus colour #3AE660 at 1.66:1 on white [EW].
- Things: ring at about 1.99:1 at 50% and about 2.91:1 at 75% on white (computed for this ledger).

**Working proposal:** a visible designed focus at 3:1 on every surface is baseline; ring style is register.

**Verdict: agree, with one refinement.** Wise shows why the gap layer is not universal. Its ring colour (ink, 19.23:1) reaches 3:1 on every surface it can land on, so no gap is needed. The two-layer ring is a technique for when one ring colour cannot contrast with every surface, for example Aesop's cream gap over dark imagery.

**Proposed baseline:** "Every interactive element has a designed focus indicator (not the UA default) that:

- reaches 3:1 against every colour adjacent to it on every surface it can land on;
- is distinct from hover;
- does not shift layout;
- works without JavaScript;
- has an inset variant for dense rows and menu items.

If one ring colour cannot reach 3:1 on every surface, add a gap layer in the surface colour."

**Moves to register:** offset outline, two-layer ring, inset ring, border swap, dashed marquee (Figma), and a travelling highlighter (Aesop).
