# Registers research: LUXURY AND RESTRAINT

Exemplars: Aesop (aesop.com, US storefront) and Apple product pages beyond the iPhone page already in `references/fifth-evidence.md` (apple.com/mac, apple.com/apple-vision-pro, apple.com/airpods-pro). A cross-family comparison with Linear and Vercel follows, built from the same measurements.

## Method

Everything below was measured on 2026-11-10. Nothing was estimated by eye unless a row says so.

1. **Shipped stylesheets.** These are the user's pagevault captures in `C:\Users\emman\Downloads\Personal\python\tools\pagevault\output\sites\`. They were read as text with scripts kept outside the capture folders, and nothing inside a capture folder was executed.
    - Aesop: `commons.css`, `header-transparent.css` and `pagedesigner.css`, served from `demandware.static/Sites-aesop-us-Site`.
    - Apple: 37 stylesheets, among them `globalheader.css`, `ac-localnav.built.css` and the `overview.built.css` file for each page.
2. **Computed values from the live sites.** A Playwright headless Chromium (the pagevault virtualenv's interpreter, running my own script) loaded each live URL at 1440x900 and at 390x844. It scrolled the full page so lazy content would render, then read `getComputedStyle` on every visible element. From that it produced:
    - histograms of type, radius, shadow, border, transition, background and text colour;
    - the chrome geometry;
    - the focus ring, by pressing Tab up to 30 times;
    - hover states;
    - counts of reduced-motion and focus-visible rules from `document.styleSheets`.
3. **Raw output.** JSON files and screenshots are in `C:\Users\emman\AppData\Local\Temp\wg-scripts\probe-out\`, `probe-out2\` and `targeted-out\`. The scripts that produced them, `probe.py` and `targeted.py`, are in `wg-scripts\`.
4. **Contrast.** Ratios use the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Alpha colours were flattened over the stated surface before computing (`wg-scripts\contrast.py`).
5. **Press and docs.** WebSearch and WebFetch were used for Work & Co's Aesop case study, the Fast Company Innovation by Design 2018 entry, and Fonts In Use.
6. **Mobbin.** Aesop has no screens on Mobbin, on either web or iOS. The Apple Store iOS app does. Mobbin images are previews, so no pixel value is taken from them.

### Caveats

- **Aesop is a re-platform.** The live aesop.com runs on Salesforce Commerce Cloud: the CSS path is `demandware.static`, and class names follow the `c-`, `l-`, `m-` and `h-` prefixes. The design Work & Co shipped in 2018 was a different build (WORKCO, FASTCO). What is measured here is today's site. Several findings below show that design decaying, and they are labelled as such.
- **Cloudflare blocked most Aesop pages.** It served a "Performing security verification" page to headless Chromium for the 390px home page, the PLP (`hand-and-body.html`) and the Library. The PDP loaded in one fresh session, and only its focus behaviour and button hover were recorded. Aesop PLP, cart, mobile and editorial values are **UNVERIFIED**; they are listed under the capture requests.
- **Apple media did not render.** WebGL and video failed in headless Chromium, so some imagery came out as solid black shapes in the screenshots. Values for text sitting on imagery are therefore unreliable, and are labelled.
- **Marketing only.** All of this is marketing and commerce web. Neither exemplar ships a web app, so app-only criteria such as row pitch and sidebar cannot be tested against them.

Tags: **Q** = QUALITY, a property expected in the other registers too. **R** = REGISTER, a choice another premium register would make differently.

## LUXURY AND RESTRAINT register

**Point of view.** The object and its material are the hero: the product photograph, the bottle, the device. The interface is a quiet, consistent frame made of very few values, reused hundreds of times. Confidence shows in what is left out: almost no chromatic colour, no resting shadows, no tracked uppercase, and few features.

### Failure mode: how this register decays into a template

**The AI tell:**

- a cream page;
- an Optima-like or thin serif display at a large size;
- tracked uppercase eyebrows, small and letterspaced;
- sharp outline buttons with an arrow;
- a muted gold or brown accent used as the link and button colour;
- a full-bleed lifestyle photograph under a centred white headline on a dark gradient;
- generous but uniform section gaps.

This keeps the surface and drops the system. The measurements say otherwise on each point:

1. **Neither exemplar uses tracked uppercase.** Visible text set in uppercase: Aesop 0 of 216 elements, Apple 0 of 1,004 across the three pages. Letter-spacing on Aesop is `normal` everywhere. Uppercase mono labels belong to the developer-tool family instead: Linear 8, Vercel 1 (LIN-LIVE, VER-LIVE).
2. **The accent does not go on actions.** Aesop's brown appears only on "Limited release" labels, and its primary button is ink.
3. **A few values carry nearly everything.** #333 is used 503 times and #fffef2 447 times. The top three font sizes cover 84 percent of text elements on Aesop and 81 to 85 percent on Apple.
4. **The states are designed.** Busy button, silhouette placeholders, a travelling focus highlighter, and field validity states (below).
5. **Features are left out.** Work & Co "pushed against the notion that any modern checkout flow should offer an option of saving products to a wishlist" (FASTCO). Today's re-platformed site has added a 24px heart-shaped wishlist button to every product tile (AE-LIVE `.c-add-to-wishlist`, AE-PDP). This is the decay happening on the exemplar itself.

**The Apple-clone variant:**

- a giant bold headline with a grey subhead;
- a blue pill on every action;
- every card at one large radius;
- glass on every surface;
- gradient text.

It drops the optical tracking table, the grey-and-black highlight hierarchy, the per-section theme flips that follow the narrative, the section-padding tokens, and the fact that selection is ink rather than blue.

**Notes by layer**

- **01 Token.** The quality is in reuse and in the steps, not in the number of tokens. There are two neutrals with three tone steps each, one alpha hairline, one functional hue for merchandising, and one each for error and valid. Measured steps: 1.08 and 1.19:1 between surfaces; 1.45:1 for the hairline.
- **03 Primitive.** One button height (48px) and three emphasis levels: ink fill, alpha outline, and cream outline on imagery. A designed busy state. A focus ring that travels between targets. Field validity states built from a 1px ring.
- **04 Brand.** The wordmark is centred and swapped per hero (a dark variant and a light variant). The brand also lives in the copy voice, in the photography and in the silhouette placeholders, not in a colour.
- **06 Mark.** "Limited release" is a word in a hue, without a pill and without a fill. The `0 4px 4px 0` radius exists in the CSS, but there is no background fill (`background: none`).
- **08 List/modal.** Overlays are a scrim plus a faint two-layer shadow, with square corners. Whether the flyout has any motion is UNVERIFIED (the CSS shows none).
- **09 Section.** A module is: an eyebrow in 14px Suisse, a 31px Zapf headline, a 14 to 16px body in #666, and a 48px outline button with an arrow. Hero copy sits over a bottom gradient to black (seen in AE-SHOT; the stops are UNVERIFIED). Section gaps were not measured; the CSS values 60, 70 and 100px exist, but which one applies is UNVERIFIED.
- **12 Chrome.** A ceremonial masthead rather than a compact one, transparent over the hero. It carries many actions, as commerce requires: search, account, cart, sign-up, stores and service.

**Notes by layer**

- **01 Token.** The glyph and fill split (text roles separate from surface roles) is the cleanest token taxonomy in the evidence. Each hue has both a glyph value and a fill value, chosen so each meets contrast in its own role (#0066cc for text, #0071e3 for fills).
- **02 Scale class.** Classes are named by role (`typography-section-headline`, `-section-body`, `-section-eyebrow`, `-drawer-caption`, `-router-headline`), with dozens per page. This agrees with the iPhone entry in fifth-evidence.
- **03 Primitive.** Pills in three heights. Hover moves one small step. Disabled is the token .42. Focus is a gap plus a ring on media controls and an outline with offset elsewhere.
- **09 Section.** Section padding is a token with a named exception (216px). Copy hierarchy inside a paragraph comes from colour (grey run, ink highlight) rather than from weight or size. The theme can flip per section (AVP).
- **11 Page.** Pages alternate #fff and #f5f5f7 bands. Media widths go up to 1800px while text stays at 400 to 820px measures.
- **12 Chrome.** A two-tier thin bar. The local nav carries exactly one action. Glass appears only once the nav sticks, and it has a no-blur fallback.

**The proxy caveat.** `generations.md` uses "count the distinct values per axis" as the measurable line between the fourth and fifth generations. On this register it points the wrong way:

- Aesop has 2 radii, 0 shadows, 5 durations and 7 sizes. That is fewer distinct values than a fourth-generation template has, and the site is premium.
- Linear has 20 radii and 14 shadows.

The part that does survive across registers is **concentration**: a few values reused at very high counts, such as #333 x503 and the top three sizes covering at least 78 percent of text. Restraint collapses axes on purpose, so value count alone cannot separate it from a template.
