# Registers research: TRUST AND FINANCE, PLAYFUL AND BOLD

Method. I downloaded the shipped stylesheets with curl and read them for custom properties and computed values:

- **mercury.com:** 4 `/_next/static/chunks/*.css`, 1,863 custom-property definitions, 470 unique.
- **app.mercury.com/login:** the logged-out app shell links 32 design-system CSS files on `cdn.mercury.com/builds/801d03d9.../static/css/` (DSButton, DSCard, DSModal, DSToast, main and others), with 615 unique custom properties.
- **wise.com/gb:** 6 `/static-assets/app/_next/static/css/*.css` (2.2 MB main file, 897 unique custom properties).
- **duolingo.com:** 187 CSS chunks listed in `js/manifest-173aabb1.js` on `d35aaqx5ub95lt.cloudfront.net`. The 172 that are not debug chunks were analysed. The colour palette comes from `js/4779-0fb1dea3.js`.
- **figma.com:** a 144 KB inline `<style>` block plus `/_netlify/_next/static/css/617e0bc399c83bea.css`.

App screens were read from Mobbin (ids given). I computed contrast ratios with WCAG relative luminance, flattening alpha onto the stated surface. Tags: **Q** = QUALITY (expected in any premium register), **R** = REGISTER (a choice another premium register would legitimately make differently). Anything not measured is marked UNVERIFIED.

Caveats:

- Marketing CSS is not app CSS. For Mercury, the app CSS is real shipped CSS, but only the components loaded by the login shell. For Wise, the marketing bundle carries the Neptune or WDS component layer that the app also uses (UNVERIFIED that the app ships the same file). For Duolingo, the CSS is the web app itself. For Figma, only the marketing site was measured; the editor is logged in.
- Mobbin images are scaled previews, so pixel sizes read from them are approximate and marked UNVERIFIED.
- The blog posts on Medium (Wise Design) returned 403 and were not read directly. Claims attributed to them come from WebSearch summaries and are marked as such.

## TRUST AND FINANCE

**Point of view.** Money is the content. Every surface exists to make an amount legible, attributable and calm, and trust is earned by showing proof (fees, rates, regulators, footnotes) in the same typographic system as the claim.

**Failure mode (the AI tell).** A navy or near-black hero with a glowing 3D credit card, a "bank-grade security" shield row, a logo cloud, Inter at 600, and amounts set in the body face with proportional figures that do not align. It copies the costume of trust (dark, blue or green, card mockups) and drops the mechanics:

- no money type role (no tabular or raised-cents treatment, no in/out/pending colour roles);
- claims with no footnote or disclosure system;
- the brand accent used as text where it fails contrast (copying Wise's bright green onto white gives 1.47:1);
- pill buttons with no busy or disabled tokens;
- one shadow on every card.

The second tell is "fintech playful": copying Wise's overshoot easing and saturated green into a context that never shows a fee or a rate.

Layer notes:

- **01 Token.** The instructive move is role tokens that survive a theme swap (12 section themes, one vocabulary). Tracking is positive at small sizes and zero at display, the inverse of the developer-tool house style.
- **03 Primitive.** Busy is a first-class state (`aria-busy:bg-surface-primary-active`). Secondary emphasis is an accent wash, not an outline. Focus treatment differs by surface (outer offset on buttons, inset on menu items).
- **06 Mark.** Status is a word chip plus a strikethrough amount, not colour alone.
- **07 Row.** The canonical finance row: right-aligned amounts with a raised-cents device, masked account identifiers, an inline category control, and a failed state shown by strikethrough.
- **09 Section.** Sections change theme (light, dark, tinted) as the narrative moves; the nav animates to match.
- **12 Chrome.** Command palette and shell are surfaced permanently (sidebar item plus floating button).

## PLAYFUL AND BOLD

**Point of view.** The interface is a toy with rules. Saturated colour, chunky physical controls and characters reward every action, while a small strict set of shapes, weights and type roles keeps the noise legible. In Figma's case, colour and jumbo shapes are the brand voice, and type and chrome stay black and white so the colour can shout.

Note: the two exemplars split by surface. Duolingo is playful in the product itself. Figma is bold in brand and marketing, while its app (Mobbin) is a quiet tool UI whose only playful trace is per-product colour coding.

**Failure mode (the AI tell).** Rainbow fills, a rounded font in bold everywhere, bouncy overshoot on every element, emoji as icons, confetti, and a 3D lip on everything including cards and inputs. It copies the costume and drops the rules:

- the lip without its press logic (`translateY` by the lip height and the shadow removed);
- colours without jobs (green must mean go or correct, blue must mean selected);
- a mascot as decoration instead of a state machine tied to user actions.

It also inherits the exemplar's own failures: white on owl green is 2.09:1, the nav label wolf is 4.48:1, the shipped CSS removes outlines globally, and none of the 172 CSS chunks has a `prefers-reduced-motion` block. The Figma version of the tell is colourful blobs on white with a black pill CTA, with no colour-pair logic (two tonal colours at one hue level), no width-axis type, and no dashed-marquee device.

## QUALITY versus REGISTER: what these four agree on

QUALITY, present in all four, or in the three that are not Duolingo-web with Duolingo's gap noted:

- semantic role tokens that a theme can redefine wholesale (Mercury 12 section themes, Wise personal and business themes plus the sentiment layer, Figma `data-theme` and colour pairs, Duolingo light and dark pairs);
- a dark theme designed per value, not inverted;
- per-state tokens (hover, active, busy or loading, disabled);
- finer weight steps (Mercury 360/420/480, Figma 330/540/550);
- type roles separate from body, including a UI label role;
- a display jump of 3× or more (Mercury, Wise, Figma);
- motion tokens named by intent, with exit faster than enter (Mercury app);
- shadow reserved for floating layers on light themes (Mercury app, Wise, Figma);
- motion tied to meaning (Wise amount resizing, Duolingo key travel, Rive characters reacting to taps);
- leading and tracking by script and language (Wise, Duolingo);
- contrast measured on the accent pair rather than the accent alone (Wise forest on bright green 9.45).

REGISTER, legitimately different across premium exemplars:

- border treatment (alpha hairline, opaque 2px, inset ring, none);
- depth recipe (luminance ladder, tinted fill, hard lip, soft blur);
- accent count and spend (one hue to 72 hues; accent on CTA or black CTA);
- neutral chroma (tinted or pure);
- tracking direction (negative, zero, positive at small sizes);
- display leading (0.85 to 1.25);
- button shape (pill, 8px, 12 to 16px with lip);
- chrome loudness (dim sidebar or loud labelled sidebar);
- row form (one-line table or card per row);
- focus treatment (two-layer ring, single ring, inset, dashed).
